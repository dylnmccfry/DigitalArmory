Frequently asked questions

My data isn't syncing between my devices

Make sure each device is signed in to the same Apple Account and that iCloud is turned on for Digital Armory (Settings → your name → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud). Sync can take a few minutes, especially for photos. You can check the status in the app under Settings → Backup.

How do I back up my records?

When you're signed in to iCloud, your armory backs up automatically to your private iCloud account. For an extra copy, open Settings → Export Inventory (CSV) and save the file somewhere safe.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, install Digital Armory and open it. Your firearms, photos and logs download from iCloud automatically.

Photos are using too much iCloud storage

Go to Settings → Photo Storage, choose Compact, and tap Shrink Existing Photos. Compact photos are usually 100–250 KB each.

How do I group identical firearms?

Firearms with the same manufacturer and model group together automatically. To group others, give them the same group name when editing. You can turn grouping off in the sort menu on the Armory tab.

How accurate is the sight-in calculator?

It estimates the bullet's path from your muzzle velocity, ballistic coefficient and sight height, assuming sea level and no wind. Use it as a starting point, enter your real ammo data, and always confirm on paper at a safe, approved range.

Which way do I turn my scope turrets?

Turn each turret in the direction the app shows. For example, if your group is high, dial DOWN. Turret markings vary between scopes, so check your scope's manual for its direction and click value.

Face ID isn't working or I'm locked out

The app lock uses your iPhone passcode as a backup. Tap Unlock and enter your passcode. You can turn the lock off in Settings → Privacy.

How do I delete my data?

Delete records in the app at any time. To remove everything from iCloud too, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, choose Digital Armory and delete its data.

Can I buy or sell firearms in the app?

No. Digital Armory is a private record-keeping tool. It doesn't sell, transfer or advertise firearms, ammunition or accessories.

Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

The short version: Digital Armory doesn't collect, sell or share your data. Everything you enter stays on your device and in your own private iCloud account, and we can't see it.

Who we are

Digital Armory ("the App") is published by Dylan McCaffrey ("we", "us"). Contact: 33.savings-voyager@icloud.com.

Information you enter

You may enter details about your firearms (such as make, model, caliber, serial numbers, purchase prices and values), photos, range sessions, maintenance records and sight-in settings. This information is stored:

  • On your device, protected by iOS data protection, and
  • In your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), if you're signed in to iCloud, so it can sync to your other devices and be restored.

Your private iCloud database belongs to your Apple Account. We have no access to it. Apple handles iCloud data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The App has no accounts, servers, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs. We don't receive, store, sell or share any personal information.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photos: used only when you choose to photograph or attach images of your own items. Photos are compressed and location data is removed before saving.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: optional, used only to lock the App. Biometric data never leaves Apple's Secure Enclave and is never available to us.

Exports

If you export your inventory as a CSV file, the file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it. Exported files aren't encrypted, so please store them securely.

Your control

You can edit or delete any record in the App at any time. Deleting the App removes its data from your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone or iPad and delete Digital Armory's data.

Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and over and isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
